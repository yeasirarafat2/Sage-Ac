# Credit Control Cycle for Streamline Media

## Overview
This document outlines the complete credit control cycle, crucial for managing payments, avoiding late fees, and maintaining cash flow.

## Credit Control Cycle Steps
1. **Customer Onboarding (Day 0)**
   - Document required: Customer Credit Application
   - Review customer creditworthiness.

2. **Order Processing (Days 1-2)**
   - Confirm order details and communicate with the customer.

3. **Invoicing (Day 3)**
   - Document required: Sales Invoice
   - Ensure timely invoicing after service delivery.

4. **Payment Terms Communication (Days 3-4)**
   - Clearly communicate payment terms to the customer.

5. **Payment Monitoring (Days 5 - due date)**
   - Monitor accounts receivable.
   - Send reminders leading up to due date:  
     - 1 week before due date.
     - 2 days before due date.

6. **Payment Collection (Due Date)**
   - Confirm payment has been received.

7. **Late Payment Follow-up (Day after due date)**
   - Contact customer regarding late payment.
   - First reminder (Day 1 post due date).
   - Second reminder (Day 7 post due date).

8. **Statutory Interest Calculation**
   - Under the **Late Payment of Commercial Debts Act 1998**, interest is calculated if payment is late.  
   - **Interest Rate**: 8% above the Bank of England base rate.  
   - Calculate:  
     \[ \text{Interest} = \frac{\text{Total Amount Due} \times 8\%}{365} \times \text{Days Late} \]  
   - Document required: Late Payment Interest Calculation Form.

9. **Final Legal Action (Optional)**
   - Engage legal services if payment is overdue beyond agreed terms.

## Documentation Required
- Customer Credit Application
- Sales Invoice
- Late Payment Interest Calculation Form

## Credit Control Communications
- Initial Communication: Upon onboarding.
- Order Confirmation: Upon order processing.
- Payment Reminder Communications: Scheduled as outlined above.

**Note**: Ensure all communications are documented and saved for future references.
